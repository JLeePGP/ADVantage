Capacity Diagnostics

    Advisor Overload RiskAUM per advisor exploded from $20.7M in 2025 to $50.7M in 2026 following a drop from 6 advisors to 3 — a 145% workload surge in a single year on a book that is 69% HNW.Systematize client segmentation and service tiers immediately so the 3 remaining advisors are not treating a $2M HNW relationship the same as a $150K account — this buys capacity without adding headcount.
  • •
    Client Erosion UnaddressedThe firm lost 36 client relationships between 2022 and 2026 (216 → 180) while reporting zero marketing infrastructure across all five years — attrition is structural, not cyclical.Build a referral activation program targeting the existing HNW client base first — 40 clients at this wealth level each have a network; one structured ask per quarter is more efficient than any cold outreach channel.
  • •
    HNW Concentration FragilityApproximately 40 HNW clients now represent ~69% of $152.2M in AUM — up from 58% in 2022 — meaning the loss of 4-5 relationships could erase $10M+ in AUM with no pipeline to absorb the shock.Introduce a formal client retention protocol for the top 20 HNW relationships — annual planning reviews, proactive outreach cadence, and next-gen family engagement — before a departure becomes a cascade.

Positioning Audits

  • •
    No Client ProfileThe homepage uses generic language ('you and your family') with no defined ideal client — HNW prospects with $1M+ in investable assets have no signal that this firm is built for them.Adding a single above-the-fold statement that names the client type (e.g., 'We work with families with $500K–$5M in investable assets navigating retirement and wealth transfer') immediately filters for fit and reduces wasted discovery calls.

    Fee-Only UnderutilizedThe fee-only structure is mentioned once in passing with no explanation of what it means or why it matters — the firm's most defensible trust signal against commission-based competitors is functionally invisible.A dedicated 3-sentence explainer on the homepage — what fee-only is, what it eliminates, and what that means for the client's interests — directly addresses the primary objection HNW prospects have when evaluating advisors.
  • •
    Zero Social ProofNo AUM figures, years in business, number of families served, testimonials, or third-party recognition appear anywhere on the site — a first-time visitor has no evidence-based reason to trust this firm over a larger branded competitor.Publishing even one credibility anchor — '30+ years serving Pacific Northwest families' or '$150M+ in assets under management' — gives HNW prospects the minimum threshold signal they need to take the next step.
