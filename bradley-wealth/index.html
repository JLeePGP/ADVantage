    Productivity CollapseAUM per advisor dropped from $70.6M in 2025 to $39.3M in 2026 — a 44% single-year decline — coinciding with the addition of a fifth advisor and the loss of 75 clients.Before adding further headcount, the firm needs a structured onboarding and client assignment model that ties new advisors to defined segments, so capacity additions translate to AUM growth rather than dilution.
  • •
    Retention System AbsentTotal clients fell from 279 in 2025 to 204 in 2026 — a 27% drop in one year — with no evidence of a formal client review cadence, segmentation protocol, or early-warning attrition trigger in place.Implementing a tiered client contact model (frequency and depth scaled to AUM tier) directly addresses the attrition pattern and protects the 52 HNW relationships that now represent 75%+ of revenue.
  • •
    Mid-Market Pipeline GoneAverage account size in 2026 is $195,550 — below the 2022 baseline of $234,041 — while HNW concentration rose, indicating the mid-market segment that once provided pipeline depth has been allowed to atrophy.Defining a formal mid-market segment ($250K–$750K) with a distinct service model and onboarding path creates a feeder tier that reduces HNW concentration risk and rebuilds AUM volume without competing for the same ultra-HNW referrals.

  • •
    No Client SignalThe homepage presents no minimum asset threshold, no ideal client description, and no segment-specific language — a $2M prospect and a $150K rollover prospect land on the same undifferentiated page.A single above-the-fold statement defining the firm's client (e.g., 'We work with families and business owners managing $500K or more') immediately filters for fit and signals to HNW referrals that they're in the right place.

    Undifferentiated Headline'Wealth & Financial Planning Firm In Scottsdale' is a category description, not a positioning statement — it communicates geography and service type but nothing about why this firm over any of the competing Scottsdale RIAs making identical claims.Replacing the headline with a statement rooted in the firm's founding story, philosophy, or a specific client outcome it's built to deliver gives HNW prospects an emotional and strategic hook that generic competitors cannot copy.
