    Productivity Masking AttritionAUM/advisor rose 32% from $34.4M in 2022 to $45.5M in 2026, but this gain is driven by losing 1 advisor and 17 clients — not by adding assets or relationships.Redirect the productivity narrative: use the $45.5M/advisor figure as proof of HNW service capacity, then build a documented referral or outreach process to convert that capacity into net new clients.
  • •
    Client Count ErosionTotal clients declined from 190 in 2022 to 173 in 2026 with AUM essentially flat at $136.4M, confirming the firm is not replacing departing clients at any meaningful rate.Establish a minimum viable lead-generation system — even a basic CRM-tracked referral program targeting existing HNW clients — to create a measurable pipeline before the next advisor or client departure creates a structural gap.
  • •
    HNW Concentration ExposureApproximately 60% of AUM has been concentrated in roughly 25% of clients every year from 2022 to 2026, now serviced by 3 advisors down from 4 — meaning each advisor's book carries disproportionate single-client departure risk.Segment the HNW book by relationship tenure and referral potential, then build a structured touchpoint cadence for the top 10-15 relationships to deepen retention and activate introductions before attrition accelerates.

    Facebook Login WallThe firm's primary web presence routes to a Facebook business page gated behind a login requirement, meaning prospects see zero brand content, service description, or credibility signals before being forced to authenticate.Launching even a minimal standalone landing page with a clear value proposition, advisor credentials, and a contact path would immediately eliminate the authentication barrier and create the firm's first functional top-of-funnel asset.
